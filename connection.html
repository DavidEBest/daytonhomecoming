Talent Connections

Dayton Homecoming recognizes that some people will require employment in Dayton before being able to relocate.

That is why we have set up a Dayton Homecoming Talent Connection* service. Tapping into our network of HR professionals and recruiters as well as Homecoming Evangelists and Connectors, we work to help give future Daytonians more visibility in their job search.

Please send your resume along with a cover letter expanding on your professional objective along with an explanation of your Dayton Homecoming story to our Talent Coordinator Peggy West at peggy@daytoncollaboratory.org. Peggy will then reach out to you to schedule a short Zoom call so we can have a full sense of what you are looking for, who you are talking to and how we can help.

*Note: Dayton Homecoming Talent Connection supports individuals looking to move back to or to Dayton as well as individuals whose spouse or partner has taken a position in Dayton. We do not support Daytonians looking for a new position.
