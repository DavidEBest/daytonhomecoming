Dayton Evangelist and Connector Registration

Dayton Homecoming is a community-based project that aims to bring former Daytonians home and inspire non-natives to call Dayton home through peer-to-peer conversations with Dayton Evangelists, storytelling, and an annual Dayton Homecoming Weekend.

Note that none of the data collected will be shared. It is intended to be used by the Dayton Homecoming team for making connections to support the Dayton Evangelists, as there may be times we want to connect a prospect who is, for example, living in the same city you did, went to the same college or has a similar interest area.
