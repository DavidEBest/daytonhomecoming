Dayton Homecoming: Get Back to Where You Belong

DAYTON HOMECOMING is designed to infuse the region with returning and new residents who can contribute to the "renaissance in progress" at a time when all rust-belt regions are struggling to attract talent and grow population. Goals are twofold:

  • Attract and retain motivated talent
  • Change perceptions about Dayton and give Dayton a “cool” factor by authentically telling our new story. Ultimately, the goal is for Dayton to become a destination people WANT to relocate to, and not just because they are from here or have been recruited for a position here.

DAYTON HOMECOMING supports the conversations Daytonians are already having with family and friends by serving as a hub of inspiration, information, and connections for:

  • Dayton Evangelists (Click here to Register as a Dayton Evangelist)
  • Those looking to relocate to the Dayton region
  • Regional stakeholders with a shared interest in community and economic development and/or a specific need to attract talent.

DAYTON HOMECOMING assists future Daytonians by making professional and community connections before and after arrival. Our network of Connectors (Click here to Register as a Connector)—navigators and connectors—have deep subject matter knowledge and/or extensive networks. We are committed to support future and new Daytonians with introductions regarding industry, non-profit, arts & culture, music, dining and restaurants, interest-areas, hobbies, faith, exercise and wellness, etc.

Homecoming - the Whys, Hows, and Whats

Why

  • A more thriving region catalyzed by returnees and transplants bringing fresh energy and new ideas
  • To spark new investment
  • To constantly reinvigorate our communities, industry, and small businesses

How

  • Peer-to-peer/relationship-based sales & marketing delivered in a personalized manner by a loose coalition of Dayton Evangelists who represent returnees, transplants and long timers/lifers
  • A core team that helps the Dayton Evangelists put together their personal “move-to-Dayton campaign”
  • A support network to assist those looking to move to Dayton, get to Dayton and get situated
  • Employer Engagement for multiple levels of partnering.

What

  • Storytelling by Dayton Evangelists with multiple distribution channels.
  • Annual Dayton Homecoming Weekend and monthly meet-up events
  • Website with all the relevant content to support someone looking to move to Dayton including comprehensive Opportunity-Talent Connection Portal
  • Connectors - individuals who can help (potential) returnees and transplants to network and make connections
  • Strong social media presence to tie it all together
